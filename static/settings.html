✉️ Email accounts

    Where do I get an app password?

    Gmail: turn on 2-Step Verification, then go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords, create one called “Dashboard” and paste the 16 letters here.

    Yahoo: Account security → Generate app password. iCloud: account.apple.com → App-Specific Passwords.

    Outlook/Hotmail: Microsoft has turned off password logins for many personal accounts. If it fails, forward that mailbox to a Gmail account and add the Gmail one.

    The dashboard only reads your mail. It never marks anything as read.

    📅 Calendly

    How do I get a token?

    In Calendly open Integrations → API & Webhooks, click “Generate new token”, name it “Dashboard” and paste it here. Free Calendly plans can do this.

    🗓️ Google Calendar

      Where do I find that address?

      On a computer, open Google Calendar settings, click your calendar under “Settings for my calendars”, scroll to Integrate calendar and copy Secret address in iCal format. No Google login is needed. Keep the link private, because anyone who has it can read the calendar.

        🎙️ Podcasts

            ✨ AI summaries (optional, free)

            Without this, each email shows its first line and the news card shows headlines. With a free Google Gemini key, you get a one-line AI summary of each email and a short news digest.

            How do I get a free key?

            Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey, sign in with Google and click “Create API key”. The free tier is plenty for a dashboard. Note that Google may use free-tier requests to improve its products, so leave this off if you don't want your email subjects and snippets sent to Google.
